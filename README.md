#Netflix-Dashboard


1 — Project Title / Headline:-


🎬 Netflix Universe: Genres, Ratings & Global Trends Dashboard
An interactive and visually stunning Power BI dashboard built to explore Netflix TV Shows data — focusing on genre distribution, ratings analysis, type breakdown, country-wise content, year-wise trends, and top-rated show highlights.



2 — Short Description / Purpose:- 


The Netflix TV Shows Dashboard is a visually themed and analytically powerful Power BI report designed to help users explore and understand the Netflix content library through data. Built on a dataset covering thousands of TV shows across multiple genres, types, and countries — this dashboard highlights key metrics like total TV shows, total genres, total countries, average rating, and the latest show added.
This tool is built for Netflix subscribers, content analysts, data enthusiasts, and Power BI learners who want to understand which genres are most popular, how content has grown over the years, and which countries produce the most Netflix shows.



3 — Tech Stack:-


* 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for building all visuals and reports
* 🗂️ Power Query – Used for cleaning and transforming the raw Netflix dataset
* 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPI cards and custom measures like Total TV Shows, Total Genres, Total Countries,              Average Rating, and Latest Show Added
* 📅 Data Modeling – Filters and slicers set up for dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals
* 🐍 Python (Pandas) – Used for initial data cleaning, null handling, and preprocessing
* 📁 File Format – .pbix for Power BI report
* 🎨 Theme – Netflix inspired dark red and black theme for immersive visual experience



4 — Data Source:- 


Source: Kaggle — https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rehanliaqat17/netfix
Dataset contains Netflix content records covering thousands of unique TV shows and movies across multiple genres, countries, and release years — including ratings, types, and image URLs.


Key Fields:

* Title – Name of the TV Show / Movie
* Type – Format of the content (TV Show / Movie / Documentary and more)
* Genre – Genre categories (Drama / Comedy / Action / Thriller and more)
* Rating – IMDB Rating of the show
* Rating_Group – Rating grouped into ranges (1-2, 2-3... 9-10)
* Country – Country where the show was produced
* Release_Year – Year the show was released on Netflix
* Image_URL – Poster image URL for each show


5- Features / Highlights:-

a — Business Problem:-


Netflix has grown into the world's largest streaming platform — with thousands of titles released across dozens of genres, types, and countries. Without a structured analytical view, it becomes very difficult to understand which genres dominate the platform, how content has grown over the years, and which shows are the highest rated. Content researchers and data analysts both need a clear and simple way to explore this massive dataset.



b — Goal of the Dashboard:-


The goal of this Netflix TV Shows Dashboard is to provide one single interactive report where anyone can:


* ✅ See total TV shows, genres, countries, average rating, and latest show using KPI Cards
* ✅ Understand genre distribution using TV Shows by Genre Donut Chart
* ✅ See content type breakdown using TV Shows by Type Pie Chart
* ✅ Track how Netflix content grew over the years using Line Chart
* ✅ Understand rating distribution using TV Shows by Rating Column Chart
* ✅ Discover country-wise content using Interactive Map
* ✅ Explore top shows using Top 10 TV Shows Table
* ✅ See the top rated show with poster image using Top Rated Show Table
* ✅ Analyze year-wise releases using Release Year-wise Shows Bar Chart
* ✅ Filter the entire dashboard by Type, Genre, Country, IMDB Rating, and Year using Interactive Slicers


c — Walk Through of Key Visuals:-


* KPI Cards (Top Row):
Show Total TV Shows, Total Genres, Total Countries, Average Rating, and Latest Show Added — gives an instant snapshot of the entire Netflix library in one glance.


* TV Shows by Genre (Donut Chart):
Shows how all Netflix content is distributed across genres — clearly highlighting which genres dominate the platform and their percentage share.


* TV Shows by Type (Pie Chart):
Breaks down all content across different formats — TV Shows, Movies, Documentaries and more — showing which format Netflix produces the most.


* TV Shows Added Over the Years (Line Chart):
Displays how Netflix content library has grown year by year — showing the massive expansion of original and licensed content over time.


* TV Shows by Rating (Clustered Column Chart):
Shows how Netflix shows are spread across different rating ranges — clearly showing which rating group has the most titles on the platform.


* TV Shows by Country (Map Visual):
Visualizes Netflix content production across the globe — highlighting which countries produce the most content available on Netflix.


* Top 10 TV Shows (Table):
Displays the top 10 Netflix shows ranked by key metrics — giving a quick view of the most prominent titles in the dataset.



* Top Rated Show (Table with Image):
Displays the highest rated Netflix show with its poster image, title, and rating — making it the most visually engaging element of the dashboard.


* Release Year-wise Shows (Clustered Bar Chart):
Shows the number of shows released each year — helping understand which years had the highest content output on Netflix.


* Interactive Slicers (5 Filters):
Allow users to filter the entire dashboard by Type, Genre, Country, IMDB Rating, and Release Year — with just one click enabling dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals.



d — Business Impact & Insights:-


* 🎭 Drama dominates Netflix — leading as the most produced genre on the platform
* 📺 TV Shows outnumber Movies — contributing the majority of all Netflix titles
* 🌍 USA leads in content production — producing the highest number of Netflix shows globally
* 📈 Netflix content grew massively after 2015 — showing rapid expansion of the streaming library
* ⭐ Average IMDB Rating reflects quality content — with most shows falling in the 6-8 rating range
* 🗓️ Content library spans multiple decades — from classic titles to the latest releases
* 🔢 Multiple genres and countries — making Netflix a truly global and diverse streaming platform
